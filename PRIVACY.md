# MK PDF privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

MK PDF is a PDF editor for Mac, Windows, Android and iPhone. This policy covers all four.

## The short version

MK PDF does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your documents stay on your device.

## What MK PDF does with your files

Everything MK PDF does happens on your device: viewing, editing, signing, recognizing text in scans, compressing, protecting and converting. Your documents, signatures, form entries and passwords are never uploaded anywhere, because MK PDF has no server to upload them to.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.** MK PDF contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
- **No data collected.** MK PDF sends nothing about you, your device or your documents to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

MK PDF keeps a few things on your device so it works the way you left it: your settings, the list of recently opened files, saved signatures, the author name you choose for comments, and unsaved changes kept for recovery. Removing the app removes them.

## Permissions

- **Camera** (phones): only when you choose Scan, to photograph paper into a PDF.
- **Photos** (phones): only when you choose a picture to add to a PDF.
- **Notifications**: to tell you when a long task (an export, text recognition) has finished. On copies downloaded from our release page, also when a new version is available.
- **Files**: MK PDF opens only the files you choose, and saves only where you tell it to.

## Internet access

- **Copies from the App Store, Mac App Store, Google Play and Microsoft Store** never connect to the internet. The store updates them.
- **Copies downloaded from our release page on GitHub** check that page for new versions. That check is an ordinary web request to GitHub: it carries no information about you or your documents, though GitHub, like any website, sees the internet address the request comes from. You can turn the check off in the Mac app's settings.

Links you tap inside a PDF, and the link to this policy, open in your web browser.

## Features provided by your device

On Apple devices, translation and the AI assistant use Apple's on-device features; MK PDF does not send your text to us. Reading aloud uses your device's own voices.

## Children

MK PDF does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
